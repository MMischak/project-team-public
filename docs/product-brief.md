# Product brief

Tinkrew helps a person assign the right kind of AI capability to the right work. Its central idea is a team: members have clear responsibilities, use suitable models and reasoning settings, share deliverables and contribute different perspectives.

The intended benefit is more deliberate allocation of capability and tokens. A routine edit should receive focused attention; a difficult problem should receive deeper reasoning; a review should have an independent perspective. The product should make those choices understandable and their actual usage visible.

## Current scope

A personal, local workspace with multiple saved projects and one shared team assigned to only one project at a time with user-selected builders/reviewers, manager proposals, sequential work, versioned handoffs, milestone gates, token accounting, discussion, recovery, selected GitHub exports and storage controls.

The user selects models and supported reasoning effort. Assignment and approval remain explicit. The application does not currently infer a task's ideal model, automatically optimize prices or demonstrate a measured reduction in token consumption.

Projects retain separate conversations, tasks, deliverables, meetings, usage and recovery. Team identities, role descriptions, models, reasoning settings and illustrated appearances are shared. Switching pauses and drains work, then opens the selected project without connecting or resuming automatically.

Characters are fictional adults in a playful animated-film style. Each member has a distinct face that remains theirs across projects.

## Direction

Improve task-to-member suggestions, compare the quality and usage of different capability choices, reduce unnecessary context and evaluate the team workflow against a single-agent baseline. Recommendations should remain explainable and respect user approval and budget boundaries.

## Success criteria

A user can explain why a member fits a task, see the tokens attributable to that work, inspect a useful result, hand it to another member and make an informed decision at the milestone. Efficiency claims require workload-specific measurements of both usage and quality.
